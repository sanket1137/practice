Proves where it is. Every single boot.

Move the screen a metre, swap the display, unplug it to a warehouse — playback stops until it re-verifies with video at its registered venue. Advertisers never pay for a screen playing to an empty room.

Learns its neighbourhood. Updates daily.

PixelOS builds the screen's audience profile automatically — zone, hub and surroundings — and refreshes it every day. When the neighbourhood changes, the screen's tags change with it:

Café · 40m Tech park · 200m Metro exit · 60m School · opened yesterday

→ now suggested to stationery & edtech advertisers

Never goes dark.

No campaign booked? The owner's own content or the default video plays. Self-booking lets owners reserve slots for their business — the glass never sits blank.

02

MATCH

Advertisers don't search. They get found.

Plan on a map, not a spreadsheet.

Pick screens by location, audience tags, availability and budget. The screens whose neighbourhoods match your customer get suggested to you — including the ones that only became relevant yesterday.

Instant booking. Instant play.

Book a slot and it can be on glass in minutes — no calls, no vendor chains, no "we'll confirm by Friday."

Every screen gets its right creative.

Each screen you select needs its own creative — and the system keeps score. Anything still missing gets flagged before launch, and Smart Adaptive Creative Fitting reshapes and corrects each one for its panel so nothing ever plays wrong.
