How it works

Simple for everyone

Two sides, one platform. Whether you own a screen or run campaigns — we make it effortless.

Screen Owners

Earn from your digital display

1

List your screen

Register your digital display with location, dimensions, and pricing. Set operating hours and slot availability.

2

Review & approve bookings

Get notified when advertisers book your slots. Preview their creative and approve or reject with one click.

3

Earn & get paid

Your Raspberry Pi player auto-plays approved ads. Earnings are tracked per impression and paid directly to your bank account.

Advertisers

Run impactful offline campaigns

1

Create a campaign

Upload your video creative (MP4), set your campaign name, budget, and target date range.

2

Discover & book screens

Browse screens on an interactive map. Filter by location, tags, price. Book specific slots for your date range.

3

Go live & track results

Pay via Razorpay. Your ad goes live on approval. Track impressions, delivery rate, and campaign performance in real-time.

Platform Features

Built for the real world

Every feature designed around how DOOH advertising actually works on the ground.

Slot-Based Booking

6 ad slots per hour, each 10 minutes. Book specific slots across any date range. Full transparency on availability with heatmap calendar view.

Real-Time Analytics

Live impression counters, delivery rates, revenue charts. Screen owners see earnings per screen. Advertisers see CPM and campaign performance.

Raspberry Pi Player

Plug-and-play hardware player for your screen. Automatically syncs, plays approved content, reports play logs. Network resilience with offline fallback.

Secure Payments

Razorpay-powered wallet system. Advertisers top up and pay per booking. Screen owners withdraw earnings directly to bank. Advance + final payout support.

Interactive Map Discovery

Advertisers browse screens on a live Leaflet map. Filter by location, tags, price range, and availability. 150+ screen tags across 17 categories.

Multi-Timezone Support

Per-screen IANA timezone config. Slot schedules and operating hours are always in the screen's local time. Perfect for distributed networks across India.
